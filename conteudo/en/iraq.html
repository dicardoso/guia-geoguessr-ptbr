Step 1 - Identifying Iraq

The coverage in Iraq is limited to a single trekker in theIraqi National Museumin Baghdad.

All of the coverage in Iraq is taken by atrekkerwith a camera similar toGen 2but with distinctly worse image quality and colour. This coverage tends to be overexposed, even becoming blindingly soat some points.

NOTE:This camera quality is sometimes referred to as Gen 1.5.

The trekker blur has a well-defined,pointedshape.

The only piece of coverage outdoors is a small patio with a diagonal walkway. In this patio you can see manydate palms.

This is the only piece of the coverage that can rarely appear on serious maps.

The inside of the museum features statues, steles, a variety ofartefacts, and a fewback rooms.
