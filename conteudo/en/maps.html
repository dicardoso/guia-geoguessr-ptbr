Map Directory

It can be quite hard to find high quality maps, as GeoGuessr's own tools for map discovery are not very good. This page is a directory of maps that Plonk It recommends, in a variety of different categories.

All maps on this page meet at least the following minimum requirements:

  • All official coverage(unless specified otherwise).
  • Not polygonal.
  • Well-considered location choice. This can mean two things:
  • Handpicked by the map author.
  • Arbitrarily picked by themap-generatoror other map generation tools, but with a well considered distribution (both in terms of country distribution and subdivision distribution within big countries).
  • A high number of locations. This is dependent on the type of map: a good world map should have tens of thousands of locations. A themed map can already be high quality with only a few hundred locations.

Pinpointable maps will have this icon.

The hand represents maps fully handpicked by the creator, and the computer represents maps made with a map-generator.

World maps

This section features World Maps that are intended for regular gameplay. While only some maps in this category are pinpointable - and therefore ideal for 25k attempts - all of them are suitable for NM and NMPZ gameplay, as well as both Country Streaks and aiming for a Highscore.

A Community World(ACW) -Map link

Handpicked • pinpointable • 105k+ locations

An Official World(AOW) -Map link

Handpicked • pinpointable • 109k+ locations

The map used in the GeoGuessr World Cup. Suitable for all game modes, it features a high amount of rounds with information close to the initial location.

An Arbitrary World(AAW) -Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 110k+ locations

Another alternative to the default World map. Its arbitrarily picked locations have the benefit of not being known by a large pool of map creators (unlike ACW).

AI Generated World(AI Gen) -Map link

Mix of handpicked & arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 135k+ locations

Most locations in this map are arbitrarily generated. However, a significant minority of locations are handpicked by the creator. These locations tend to be on the harder side, giving this map its reputation of being fairly difficult.

An Improved World(AIW) -Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 110k+ locations

A highly replayable handpicked map. It has a distribution that favours big countries more than usual. Location diversity is high, including locations such as city locations with a lot of info and remote rural dirt roads. The map is meant to be played in the No Move/NMPZ formats. There is also aversion of this mapwithout shitcam locations.

A Moving World(AMW) -Map link

Handpicked • pinpointable • 100k+ locations

A highly replayable, handpicked map, created by a team of over 70 experienced mappers. As it is optimised for Moving games, the locations will be no more than 60 seconds away from a useful clue.

A Varied World(AVW) -Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 100k+ locations

A well balanced map with a high variety of location types. This map features a lot of remote recognizable places but doesn’t forget about urban areas.

A Souvlaki World(ASW) -Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 110k+ locations

A map with an amazing location diversity and a plethora of interesting locations while still retaining enough representative and challenging locations to be a competitive viable map.

Supersonic-Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 110k+ locations

Supersonic uses every trick in the book to achieve the highest ratio of interesting locations possible. For example, this map avoids main roads, overrepresents dirt roads and locations near a coast line or at high altitude.

An Arbitrary Rural World(AARW) -Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 109k+ locations

A map that used a clever generation method to only include rural locations. Ideal for those looking for an accessible but still challenging rural map.

Dirty World(DW) -Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 79k+ locations

A map focusing on dirt/unpaved roads. The locations are arbitrarily picked by the generator in a somewhat similar fashion to An Arbitrary Rural World.

A Rural World(ARW) -Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 72k+ locations

A very rural map with a selection of hard and sometimes baity locations mixed with the regular rural vibe of the countries. Locations are panned to avoid any car meta in NMPZ.

A Competitive World(ACOW) -Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 109k+ locations

A competitive focused map with a generating method that focuses on rural locations and dirt roads but still presents a significant urban location pool. Country distribution is in part done considering how enjoyable the countries are considered to guess.

A Pro World-Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 110k+ locations

This map leans very heavily into big countries and has an above average amount of rural locations, but you will still see civilization from time to time. Intended for advanced players and team games.

A Rainbolt World-Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 110k+ locations

A map very similar to the previous one but with a more moderate bias towards big countries.

Less-Extreme Regionguessing(LERG) -Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 110k+ locations

If you want to practice your region guessing in countries big enough that clicking middle just doesn’t cut it, this map is perfect for you. It includes the 30 biggest countries with extensive coverage in street view.

Extreme Regionguessing(ERG) -Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 105k+ locations

If you find South Africa, Nigeria, or Argentina too small, perhaps Extreme Regionguessing is the map for you. After playing this map that includes equal amounts of locations in Russia, USA, Brazil, Canada, Australia, Indonesia and Mexico you will never groan when you get a big country with high multipliers.

A Tweaked World-Map link

Handpicked • pinpointable • 15k+ locations

A handpicked map focused on moving games, with confirmed information within less than 3 minutes of moving. If you are looking to hone your 5k skills this map may just be perfect for you.

An Extensive Urban World-Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 107k+ locations

Other world maps

This section features world maps that have some sort of quirk, often making them sub-optimal for competitive games. Nevertheless, these maps are still well-made and offer a different type of challenge.

A Skewed World-Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 63k+ locations •NMPZ Only

A map with a unique concept: all locations are facing away from the road in a roughly perpendicular angle, making many locations in this map extremely difficult. Should only be played on NMPZ.

Sidetrek-Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 110k+ locations •NMPZ Only

This maps presents a very unique concept. It mixes locations panned to the side of the road and regular locations, all of them handpicked to be interesting, baity, or very regionguessable.

A Totally Normal Zoom World-Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 37k+ locations •NMPZ Only

You may not be able to play GeoGuessr through a telescope, but this map sure makes you feel like you do.

AI Populated World(AI Pop) -Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 100k+ locations

A map that used population statistics to decide how many locations to allocate to each country and its subdivisions. In practice this means that highly populated countries are significantly overrepresented compared to other maps.

AI Degenerated World(AI Degen) -Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 110k+ locations • NMPZ Recommended

Tired of seeing Russia and Indonesia every few games? This map has the answer with the exact same amount of locations in almost every country and territory. Locations are panned away from big car metas, so playing NMPZ can bring an extra challenge.

Terminus-Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 90k+ locations

End of coverage locations can be some of the most interesting locations in Street View. This map only contains that type of location, and can thus be extremely interesting and challenging, particularly when playing NMPZ, as all locations are panned away from the coverage.

Intersectionguessr-Map link

Arbitrarily picked • pinpointable • 100k+ locations

Every location in this map is placed at an intersection. Try this map if you are looking for a different pinpointing challenge than the usual handpicked locations of ACW and AOW.

GeoTime-Map link

Arbitrarily picked • not pinpointable • 127k+ locations

Another map with an interesting twist on the usual location distribution: all locations are at least 10 km away from each other. This means that bigger countries are (very) over-represented, and smaller countries under-represented. Ideal for those who like region-guessing big countries.

A Curated Planet-Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 12k+ locations • NMPZ Recommended

This map features rounds with panning such that the country is gettable but car meta and text are hidden from view. Ideal for NMPZ and Chatguessr games.

A Pinpointable World-Map link

Handpicked • pinpointable • 3k+ locations

A map made for getting perfect scores without moving. You will always have enough information at the initial location to pinpoint exactly where you are.

Geodetective-Map link

Handpicked • pinpointable • 500+ locations

Another handpicked map made for getting perfect scores No Moving.

I Saw The Sign-Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 2k+ locations

Locations in this map will always have the town or city directly in view.

A Rural Trekker World-Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 6k+ locations

Locations from remote official trekkers all over the world. Forget about infrastructure and use the landscape and vegetation to guess where in the world you are.

Plonk It-Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 6k+ locations

This map collects every location shared in the Plonk It Discord server. Hard rounds, gorgeous views, people waving at the Google Car and a lot of animals, anything a Plonk It member thought was interesting enough to share goes in this map.

Plonk It Extreme-Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 600+ locations

A select collection of the hardest and most extreme locations shared in the Discord server, prepare yourself to go wrong continent in this map.

A Wonderful World-Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 11k+ locations • NMPZ Recommended

This map covers a wide range of interesting-looking locations and is made with NMPZ gameplay in mind. While many rural locations will be quite hard to guess, you can be sure to be entertained by the view.

Unofficial coverage maps

While official coverage is mandatory for any serious competitive map, there are a few high quality maps made entirely with photospheres. Forget about all your knowledge of camera and coverage meta and have fun with these interesting maps.

A Drone World - Bird’s-eye view-Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 34k+ locations

Tired of looking at rounds from the ground? This map features aerial photospheres all over the world. From busy cities to the highest peaks, this map will bring a new perspective to your games.

Geoguessr in 2069-Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 1k+ locations • NMPZ Recommended

This map uses high quality photospheres to simulate the GeoGuessr experience including many countries without official coverage. Recommended to play NMPZ.

An Unofficial Street World-Map link

Handpicked • not pinpointable • 5k+ locations

Another attempt at a world map made entirely with unofficial coverage where every location is on a road.

GeoDetective World 🌍-Map link

Handpicked • pinpointable • 5k+ locations

This worldwide version of the GeoDetective concept uses mostly photospheres (although it also has some official coverage) that are high quality and compass accurate. Almost every location should be pinpointable, but some of them may be extremely time consuming or even impossible to find.
