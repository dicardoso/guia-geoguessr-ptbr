The Plonk It Guide to GeoGuessr

Welcome to thePlonk It Guide to GeoGuessr! This guide is a collection of tips and tricks to help you improve your GeoGuessr skills.

To open a country page, click on the country on the map above or use the country list below.

This is astep-by-stepguide, with each page consisting of three sections:

  • Identifying the country
  • Regional and subdivision-specific clues
  • Spotlight: very specific clues

Note: The guide only includes countries withofficial Google street viewand their official coverage. Photospheres and Ari coverage won’t be included.

In each country, you will have numerous images of clues, accompanied by instructive descriptions.Hints beside the images give you a deeper explanation or draw comparisons to other countries.

To take a closer look at a clue, simply click on the image to get redirected to a Google Maps location showing it.

Please use this form to report inaccuracies, suggest new clues or give general feedback about our content.

If you’re interested in becoming part of our guide editing team, please fill out the form below.
